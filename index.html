Where is my data kept?

Everything you enter is kept on this phone, inside this app. It is not sent anywhere and it does not copy itself to other devices. Clearing the browser's data, removing the app or switching to a new phone erases it, so make a backup now and then. To move to another phone, make a backup here and restore it there.
